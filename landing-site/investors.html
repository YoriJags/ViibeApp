THE MARKET STRUCTURE

An intel market, not a listings app

  • Scouts supply the readings. Three seconds, from inside the geofence, weighted by how reliable that scout has proven to be over time.
  • Venues are the first buyers. An owner can see their own floor; they are standing on it. What they cannot see is whether Saturday was good for a Saturday, whether the ₦500,000 DJ did anything, or how many people considered them at 10pm and went three streets over. We sell them that.
  • Assistants are the endgame. Ask Siri, ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini where is good tonight and the answer has to come from somewhere. Nobody holds this signal for African cities. Our agent API and MCP server run in production now.
WHY THE SIGNAL IS DEFENSIBLE
DECAY-HONEST

Readings expire in minutes. A venue packed at 11 does not stay packed on the map at 2, and a quiet map is allowed to look quiet.

PRESENCE-VERIFIED

Ratings and charges are geofence checked. Taps from outside the room carry a fifth of the weight and can never drive a peak.

CORROBORATION-GATED

The top state fires city-wide alerts, so it demands five present scouts, a hot check and a ten minute hold. A group chat cannot manufacture it.

SCENE-AGNOSTIC

The engine has no idea what a nightclub is. Concerts, festivals and street events are configuration rather than rebuilds.

WHY NIGHTLIFE, WHY LAGOS

Every horizontal platform won a vertical first. Nightlife earns that slot here for three unglamorous reasons. Its energy decays in minutes, which is exactly when live information is worth paying for. A buyer already exists tonight, spending heavily on promotion with no way to check what it bought. And it is where the habit of reporting forms. Lagos happens to be one of the world's great nightlife cities carrying the world's thinnest data coverage.

THE PROOF PLAN · RUNNING NOW

Four Fridays, one corridor, falsifiable

Nobody is asked to imagine adoption here. The rollout is a four weekend experiment in the Lekki and Victoria Island corridor, Fridays 9pm to 3am, with 30 paid founding scouts, 10 or more venues, a Big Screen on an anchor venue's wall, and two merchant pilots receiving Lift Reports. One number decides it: do this weekend's scouts come back next weekend. Every figure lands on this page as it happens.
