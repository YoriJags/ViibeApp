Lagos already reads energy. It just reads it late.

Everybody here knows the difference between a place that is full and a place that is going. The problem is how that knowledge travels: a promoter who says it's mad every single night, a story filmed an hour ago, a friend's verdict that lands after you've already paid at the door.

In Lagos traffic, acting on the wrong one costs you an hour and a cover charge.

VIIBE doesn't invent that signal. It takes the one the city already runs on and makes it live.

Every reading comes from inside the room, and none of it lasts.

  1. 1

    Someone is there

    Their phone confirms they're inside the venue. You can't read a room you're not standing in.

  2. 2

    Three seconds

    How alive it feels, how full it is, how fast the door is moving. That's the whole reading.

  3. 3

    Then it fades

    Readings lose their weight within minutes, because the feeling does. A room that was going at eleven doesn't stay lit on the map at two.
