ILLUSTRATION OF THE LIVE APP
WHY YOU CAN TRUST IT

A sensor network made of people

Three stages between a room's energy and your map. Each one exists to keep the signal honest.

01 · SENSE

Scouts confirm the room

A 3-second vibe check (energy, crowd, the door) accepted only from inside the venue. You can't rate a room you're not in.

02 · WEIGH

Reputation is earned

Every signal carries its scout's credibility, built from a track record of accurate calls. Being right compounds; noise fades out.

03 · DECAY

The truth expires

Readings fade in minutes, like real energy. A place that was packed at 11 doesn't stay "packed" on the map at 2. The map keeps up with the night.
